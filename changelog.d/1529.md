- A results difference between two scans can now be attributed. The new
  read-only `scan-config-diff` (CLI `seohead scan-config-diff --before a.sqlite
  --after b.sqlite`, MCP `seo_scan_config_diff`) reads both scans' recorded
  effective configurations from their headers — the sites are never re-read —
  and reports a field-by-field diff over the manifest's dotted setting paths:
  changed settings with both values, added, removed, an identical flag, and how
  many settings were compared. Identical configurations report no differences;
  a free-form map such as `http.headers` compares as one leaf, exactly as the
  manifest records it.
