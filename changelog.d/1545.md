- `HEADING_SKIP` now flags heading-order jumps in both directions, completing the
  heading-sequence check: a content heading that skips a level down (H2 -> H4) and one
  that jumps back up past an open section (H4 -> H2) are the same defect -- the outline
  steps over a section that was never opened or never closed. A one-level return
  (H3 -> H2) closes the current section and starts a sibling, which is correct nesting
  and stays clean, matching the SF 24.3 `axe.heading_order` behaviour the check was
  modelled on. Evidence rows carry from/to levels and texts for both directions.
